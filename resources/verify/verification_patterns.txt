# Verification Patterns Report

Summary of patterns discovered in feature_compatibility and cumulative_mismatch analyses.

---

## 1. Feature Priority Pattern

**Finding:** Care and light mismatches have the most detrimental effect on plant survival.

**Survival data (match − mismatch):**
  care:   0.180 (highest)
  light:  0.142 (second)
  climate: 0.132
  zone:   0.127
  water:  0.127
  soil:   0.094 (lowest)

**Model alignment:** The model encodes this priority. Care and light rank 1 and 2 in both survival drop and model score drop. A mismatch in care (e.g., user care level below plant need) or light (user light vs plant ideal/tolerated) is correctly penalized most heavily.

**Note:** Water and zone are under-weighted by the model relative to their survival impact.

---

## 2. Cumulative Mismatch Penalty

**Finding:** Multiple small mismatches together are worse than the sum of their individual effects.

**Oracle:** MISMATCH_DECAY = 0.87 → final_score = base_score × 0.87^num_mismatches
  1 mismatch: 0.87× (13% drop)
  4 mismatches: 0.87⁴ ≈ 0.57 (nearly halves the score)
  6 mismatches: 0.87⁶ ≈ 0.44

**Interpretation:** A plant slightly off on one dimension might be fine; slightly off on four dimensions gets hit with ~0.57 multiplier. The compound penalty means each additional imperfect feature multiplies the damage.

**Model alignment:** The model should learn that mean score decreases as num_mismatches increases, with a compound (non-linear) effect rather than a simple additive drop.
